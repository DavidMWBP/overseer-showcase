Write commits as Conventional Commits: `<type>(<scope>): <subject>`. Types: feat, fix, refactor, test, docs, chore, style, perf, build, ci. Lowercase type, optional scope, imperative subject, no trailing period, header at most 72 characters; add a blank line and a body when the subject is not enough.
